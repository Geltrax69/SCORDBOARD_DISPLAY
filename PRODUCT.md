# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Tournament owners, administrators, venue operators, scorers, and display operators running live sepak takraw events. They often work under time pressure on laptops, phones, projectors, and shared venue networks and need fast, dependable setup and scoring workflows.

## Product Purpose

ScoreCast prepares tournaments, courts, teams, and player rosters; connects scorer devices; operates live match scoring; and drives broadcast displays. Success means organizers can move from registration data to a correctly configured live event with minimal manual entry and no ambiguity about current match state.

## Brand Personality

Focused, athletic, and reliable. The interface should feel like professional event operations software: calm under pressure, legible at a glance, and confident without unnecessary spectacle.

## Anti-references

Avoid playful consumer-app styling, ornamental sports graphics, decorative glass effects, excessive glow, and unfamiliar controls. Avoid low-contrast labels, tiny text, or animation that slows an operator during event setup.

## Design Principles

1. Put the current operational task and next action first.
2. Make live, pending, warning, and error states unmistakable without relying on color alone.
3. Prefer review and confirmation before bulk or destructive changes.
4. Keep setup workflows compact, predictable, and usable on a small laptop.
5. Preserve a consistent component vocabulary across dashboard, scorer, and display controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard operation. Support reduced motion, visible focus states, descriptive labels, and status indicators that remain understandable for color-blind users. Maintain practical touch targets on mobile scorer surfaces.
